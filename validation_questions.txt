User validation script

Target users:
College students / fresh graduates preparing for technical interviews.

Before:
1. How often do you practice interviews?
2. What is the hardest part of interview preparation?

After the MVP:
3. How useful was InterviewPilot? (1–5)
4. Did the feedback feel actionable? (1–5)
5. Would you use it again? (Definitely / Probably / Not sure / No)
6. What was the most useful part?
7. What should we change?
8. Would you recommend it to another student? (Yes/No)

Metrics to report:
- Number of people invited
- Number who started
- Number who completed
- Completion rate
- Average usefulness score
- % who said Definitely/Probably
- Top 3 recurring user comments

Never fabricate numbers. If the sample is small, say so.

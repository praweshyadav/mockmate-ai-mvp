# 🤖 MockMate AI
### Your Personal AI Interview Coach

MockMate AI is an AI-powered interview practice tool designed to help college students prepare for real interviews.

It simulates a short **5-question interview**, allows users to answer through **voice or text**, and provides **instant AI-generated scoring and structured feedback** after each answer.

## 🎯 Problem

Many students prepare for interviews by reading question lists or practicing alone, but they often don't get:

- A realistic interview experience
- Immediate feedback on their answers
- Clear guidance on how to improve
- An easy way to practice speaking their answers

MockMate AI provides a quick and interactive way to practice interviews and improve with every attempt.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🤖 AI Interviewer
MockMate AI asks interview questions one at a time based on the user's selected target role.

### 🔊 Voice Questions
Users can use the speaker button to hear the AI interviewer read the question aloud.

### 🎙️ Voice Answers
Users can click **Speak** and answer the interview question verbally.

Their speech is converted into text.

### ⌨️ Text Answers
Users who prefer typing can type their answer directly into the answer box.

### ✏️ Editable Voice Transcription
After speaking, the converted text appears in the answer box and can be edited before submission.

### 📊 AI Feedback
After submitting an answer, MockMate AI provides:

- Score out of 10
- What worked
- What needs improvement
- A better version of the answer
- One actionable next tip

### 🏆 Interview Summary
After completing all 5 questions, users can review their overall interview performance.

### 💬 Product Feedback
Users can rate the usefulness of the experience, indicate whether they would use it again, and provide improvement suggestions.
